Faithwell Privacy Policy

Your personal reading and reflection data stays in the app's local storage.

Effective September 29, 2026

Who this policy covers

This policy describes the current Faithwell: Bible & Prayer iPhone app, its browser preview, optional support correspondence, and this support website. The developer is Mikhail Baklashov. Questions can be sent to mike.baklashov@gmail.com.

Information stored on your device

Faithwell uses local browser or webview storage for:

  • Saved passages and favorite listening selections.
  • Your reading place and completed readings or journey days.
  • Prayer entries, their dates, and whether you mark them answered.
  • Reflection notes and date-specific evening notes.

This information supports the reading, listening, progress, and journaling features you choose to use. The app does not send it to the developer, a Faithwell server, an advertising service, or an AI service. Faithwell does not provide user accounts, a cloud journal, or account-based synchronization.

Prayers and notes may contain sensitive information about beliefs, health, or personal circumstances. You decide what to enter. Faithwell does not add application-level encryption to local storage and is not a secure medical-record system.

Retention, deletion, and backups

Local information remains until you remove it, clear the site's storage, remove the app's data, or the device or browser clears it. The developer has no server copy to access or recover.

You can delete individual prayer entries, remove saved items, and clear and save an empty reflection or evening note. To remove all app data on an iPhone, use Settings > General > iPhone Storage > Faithwell > Delete App. Offloading the app retains documents and data. For a browser preview, clear that site's data.

Faithwell does not operate a backup service. Your operating system, device backups, or cloud-backup settings may retain or restore app data independently. Manage those backups separately. Deleting the app does not promise deletion from every backup, and restoring a backup may restore earlier data.

Browser-preview data does not automatically migrate to an iPhone installation. Keep a separate copy of important writing before removing or changing your app or browser data.

Accounts, analytics, and media

The current app has no sign-in, advertising, third-party analytics SDK, or app-operated backend. It does not track users across other companies' apps or websites, and it does not sell local journal or reading data.

The Bible text, fonts, photographs, narration, music, and reflection videos are bundled with the iPhone app. Narration and nature sounds were generated during production, not from your personal entries. Playing them does not send prayers or notes to ElevenLabs or request live speech generation.

The app does not request access to your microphone, camera, contacts, health records, or precise location. Your device or Apple may handle operating-system diagnostics, backups, and App Store services under their own settings and policies.

When you contact support

If you choose to email the developer, your email address, message, and any details or attachments you send are received through email services. These are separate from the app's local notes; Faithwell does not automatically attach journal entries or reading history.

Support correspondence is used to answer your request, investigate problems, and keep records needed to handle the issue. Email providers process and may retain messages according to their services and policies. Do not send private prayer entries, medical records, passwords, or payment information unless genuinely necessary for a request; ordinary app support should not require them.

You may ask to access, correct, or delete support correspondence by emailing the address above. Requests will be handled as applicable law requires; some records may need to be retained for legal or security reasons. The developer cannot delete or retrieve app data that exists only on your device.

This support website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. The site itself has no advertising, analytics scripts, contact form, or tracking cookies. Ordinary page requests still reach the hosting provider, which may process information such as IP addresses and request metadata for operating and securing the service.

Hosting is governed by the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Contact links open your email application; they do not upload app data through this website.

External links and Apple testing

If you open a publisher, health-resource, support, or other external website from Faithwell, that service receives the normal information associated with your browser request. Its privacy practices apply. The app does not append your private notes to these links.

If you use TestFlight, Apple may collect testing and diagnostic information and share feedback or crash information with the developer under TestFlight's terms and privacy practices. Information you deliberately include in feedback or screenshots can be visible to the developer. Review screenshots before sending them.

See Apple's privacy information for its services. This policy does not replace the policies of Apple, your email provider, or external websites.

Children and sensitive information

Faithwell does not provide accounts or forms designed to collect children's personal information. Its complete Bible and spiritual-support material can include mature topics; parents and guardians should consider the app's content and App Store age rating.

If a child has sent personal information through support email, a parent or guardian can contact the developer to request its removal, subject to applicable legal requirements.

Changes and questions

This policy will be updated when Faithwell's privacy practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy. New features such as accounts, synchronization, or analytics would require an updated explanation of the information involved.

For questions or privacy requests, contact mike.baklashov@gmail.com or visit Faithwell Support.
