Faithwell Support

Help with reading, listening, and the things you save.

Contact the developer

Faithwell is developed by Mikhail Baklashov. For app support or privacy questions, email mike.baklashov@gmail.com.

Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, and a short description of the problem. Screenshots can help, but remove private prayer entries and other personal information first. Do not send passwords, medical records, or payment information.

During TestFlight testing, you can also send feedback through Apple's TestFlight app.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. The current app has no sign-in, user account, or in-app subscription. Faithwell does not provide an account-based backup or cloud-sync service.

Where are my prayers and notes stored?

Saved passages, favorites, reading progress, prayer entries, and reflection notes are stored locally in the app's webview on your device. They are not sent to a Faithwell server. Notes created in the browser preview do not automatically transfer to the iPhone app.

Faithwell does not add its own encryption to this storage. Device protections and operating-system backups are controlled by your device and account settings. Keep a separate copy of anything important; the developer cannot recover your local notes.

How do I delete saved information?

Use the delete control on a prayer entry to remove it. Use an item's save control again to remove it from your saved items. To remove a reflection or evening note, clear its text and save the empty note.

To remove all local app data on an iPhone, use Settings > General > iPhone Storage > Faithwell > Delete App. Offload App is different: it retains documents and data. Manage device or iCloud backups separately; restoring a backup may restore older app data.

For a browser installation or preview, clear site data for that browser origin. These actions can permanently remove notes. There is no Faithwell account to delete or server copy for the developer to erase.

Why can't I hear a recording?

Start playback in the app and check the media volume, the player's volume, and your selected speaker, Bluetooth device, or headphones. Pause any video before trying audio again. If the problem continues, close and reopen the app and note which recording was affected.

The narration and reflection videos are prerecorded and bundled with the app. No speech-generation account or credits are required to play them. Please report issues with screen lock, interruptions, or switching audio devices; background playback and system media controls should not be assumed.

Is the entire Bible narrated?

The complete 66-book World English Bible, Protestant Edition, is readable. The current release includes 57 prerecorded reflections, prayers, and daily listening selections, plus 33 reflection videos. It does not include a narration of every Bible chapter.

Scripture is distinguished from Faithwell's original commentary. The reader links to the publisher's source edition and footnotes.

Does the monthly journey repeat?

Yes. The same curated journey adapts to each month's length. Completion and evening notes are stored separately for each date, and days remain available to revisit or open ahead of time.

Can I use Faithwell for medical or crisis support?

Faithwell offers spiritual companionship, not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or a promise of healing. Continue working with qualified health professionals. Support email is not monitored as an emergency or crisis service.

If you or someone else is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services. In the United States, call or text 988 for crisis support. Outside the United States, use your local crisis service.
